Heat & Water · Monsoon waterlogging

Delhi's drains don't fail suddenly.
They fill.

A drain silts up over weeks — a bag, then silt, then the channel narrows — and nobody notices until a cloudburst puts a metre of water across the road. DrainWatch reads the drain from the outside and watches its choke index climb toward the flood line, so the crew is sent before it blocks, not after.

channel = waste + water
choke index = waste / channel  ∈ [0, 1]
≥ 0.70 → dispatch a crew
